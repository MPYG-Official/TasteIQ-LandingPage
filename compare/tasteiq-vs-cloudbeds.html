TasteIQ vs Cloudbeds: unified hotel OS vs PMS leader

Cloudbeds is a trusted PMS and channel manager for rooms. TasteIQ is an all-in-one hotel OS that manages rooms, events, and restaurant-grade F&B in one platform. This guide clarifies strengths, overlap, and when hotels keep both during migration.

Important clarity: TasteIQ includes a native PMS

TasteIQ is a unified hotel operating system with native PMS capabilities: room reservations, rates, availability, front desk, night audit, and housekeeping, plus full F&B operations. Cloudbeds remains a strong PMS and channel ecosystem for independent hotels, especially where channel distribution depth and brand familiarity matter.

Hotels that want one platform for rooms, events, and restaurant-grade dining use TasteIQ as their primary OS. Others keep Cloudbeds for channel preferences while deploying TasteIQ for deeper F&B workflows and unified reporting. Both approaches can be valid depending on the property’s migration timeline.

TasteIQ also supports room-charge workflows and can align with existing PMS processes during transitions. This lets front desk teams stay stable while F&B teams upgrade to restaurant-grade speed and inventory control.

Where Cloudbeds wins

Cloudbeds is a comprehensive PMS and channel manager. If you need deep channel connectivity, a large partner ecosystem, and brand familiarity for independent hotels, Cloudbeds is built for that. It remains a strong choice for properties prioritizing distribution breadth and a mature PMS workflow.

Cloudbeds also provides valuable reporting for occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, and channel performance. These metrics are essential for revenue management and are outside the scope of any F&B system. If your goal is to optimize room revenue and distribution, Cloudbeds is the right tool.

Where TasteIQ fits in the hotel stack

TasteIQ is the unified hotel OS for rooms, events, and dining. It brings restaurant-grade speed and control to outlets: fast billing, KOT routing, inventory tracking, and branded ordering for guests. It also supports hotel-specific workflows such as banquet billing, room service tickets, and multi-outlet menu governance.

Hotels often find that PMS dining modules are designed for posting charges to rooms rather than running a full restaurant. That leads to slower service, limited menu control, and weak inventory visibility. TasteIQ resolves these gaps while offering native PMS capabilities for rooms when you want one system.

OTA dining and guest ordering story

Cloudbeds is excellent for room OTAs; it is not designed for dining discovery. TasteIQ fills the dining side with branded QR ordering, takeaway, and delivery menus tailored to each outlet. This gives hotels a direct channel for guest ordering and a consistent dining experience across on-property venues. The two systems can coexist: Cloudbeds drives room occupancy while TasteIQ drives F&B revenue per guest.

Pricing bands and migration risk

Cloudbeds pricing is typically tied to rooms and occupancy. F&B modules can be bundled or added on, but they are not priced as a dedicated dining system. TasteIQ’s guarantee plan applies while migrating: ₹0 / $0 until your current subscription ends, then about 15% less than your current provider. That structure allows hotels to modernize dining and rooms without double-paying or risking downtime during high season.

When you need both systems

Some full-service hotels run Cloudbeds for distribution while using TasteIQ as the operating system for rooms and dining. This is common when a property wants Cloudbeds’ channel ecosystem but prefers TasteIQ’s unified PMS + F&B workflow for day-to-day operations.

If you are a boutique hotel with one small cafe, a PMS dining module may be enough for now. The moment you add a second outlet, banquets, or room service scale, the case for a dedicated F&B system becomes strong. TasteIQ’s outlet-level inventory and reporting make it easier to manage cost controls and reduce wastage across kitchens.

Implementation approach that preserves guest experience

Hotels should avoid large-scale migrations during peak seasons. TasteIQ can be introduced in one outlet first, then expanded to restaurants, bars, and banquets. Menus can be captured quickly from photos, and existing POS devices can be reused. This lets the F&B team adopt the new system without impacting front-desk operations.

Because TasteIQ can run alongside Cloudbeds during a phased rollout, the property can keep channel workflows stable while modernizing dining and gradually moving rooms if desired.

Reporting: rooms + dining in one dashboard

Cloudbeds provides room and channel analytics; TasteIQ provides unified room and F&B analytics. Together, they give a full view of guest value: room revenue, dining revenue, and outlet performance. For hotel owners and GMs, this combination helps identify which outlets drive upsell, which menus raise contribution margin, and where inventory leakage occurs.

Compliance and multi-property operations

Hotels operating across cities or countries need systems that can handle different tax structures. TasteIQ supports multi-tax and multi-currency configurations for rooms and F&B outlets, which is important for Indian GST as well as multi-geo hotel groups. Cloudbeds handles room taxes and regulatory reporting on the PMS side. Using both systems allows each team to remain compliant without forcing dining workflows into a room-centric tool.

If your property group includes resorts, city hotels, and boutique properties, a flexible F&B system is crucial. TasteIQ makes it easier to maintain consistent menu standards and inventory controls across properties while still letting each outlet customize menus for local tastes.

Hardware freedom and outlet agility

Hotel dining outlets change frequently: new pop-ups, seasonal menus, pool bars, or temporary banquets. TasteIQ’s device-agnostic approach makes these changes easy because you can use existing tablets or laptops without waiting for specialized hardware. This flexibility lowers capital expense and lets F&B managers experiment with new revenue opportunities faster.

Cloudbeds is still the right home for room operations, but it is not intended to manage a dynamic restaurant floor. The combination allows you to modernize dining without any disruption to the front desk and revenue management teams.

Hotel F&B modernization checklist

Use this checklist to decide whether it is time to add a dedicated F&B operating system.

  • Do you manage more than one outlet or offer banquets?
  • Is kitchen routing slow or manual during peak hours?
  • Are inventory wastage and recipe costs hard to track?
  • Do you need branded QR ordering or takeaway menus?
  • Can your PMS produce outlet-level F&B performance reports?
  • Do you want to launch pop-up dining outlets quickly?
  • Is your team double-paying during POS migrations today?
