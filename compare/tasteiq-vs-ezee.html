Decision checklist for hotel operators

Hotels choosing between a PMS module and a dedicated F&B system should focus on the daily realities of dining operations. If your outlets are busy, menus change often, or banquets drive revenue, a dedicated system can unlock better reporting and faster service.

  • Number of dining outlets and distinct menus.
  • Need for outlet-level inventory and recipe tracking.
  • Speed required to update menus and pricing.
  • Volume of room service and banquet orders.
  • Staff training time for new cashiers and stewards.
  • Desire to keep PMS unchanged while upgrading F&B.

If multiple points above resonate, TasteIQ’s unified PMS + F&B approach tends to deliver the best balance—while eZee can still be retained for channel preferences during transition.

Migration notes for hotels

Hotels that keep eZee Absolute for channel depth typically start TasteIQ with one outlet (for example, the café or all-day dining restaurant). Upload menu photos, configure GST, connect printers, and run in parallel for a week. Once billing, inventory, and reports align, expand to other outlets such as the bar, room service, and banquet operations, and migrate rooms when ready.
