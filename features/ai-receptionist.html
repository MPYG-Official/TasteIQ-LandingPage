AI Receptionist that answers hospitality calls 24x7

TasteIQ AI Receptionist is an add-on for restaurants, cafes, boutique hotels, and properties that want every call answered. It handles common questions about hours, directions, menu items, and table inquiries, and escalates complex requests to your team so hospitality stays human.

What it does

A calm, consistent voice on every inbound call

Guest calls are about reassurance, clarity, and speed. The AI receptionist handles predictable questions in seconds and routes nuanced issues to your team, so no caller is left waiting or sent to voicemail.

Designed for hospitality, not call centers. The AI receptionist is trained on your operating hours, menus, reservation policies, and dining offerings. It answers in a natural, professional tone and knows when to hand off to a human. It is not a replacement for hospitality, it is the first line of response when your team is busy or offline.

  • Answers inbound calls 24x7 with consistent information
  • Handles menu queries, timing questions, table availability inquiries, and directions
  • Routes complex requests to humans via call transfer, callback tasks, or WhatsApp
  • Summarizes missed-call intent so your team can follow up faster
Restaurant use cases

For restaurants and cafes: fewer missed calls, fewer repeated questions

Phone calls peak exactly when the floor is busiest. The AI receptionist keeps your team focused on service while still giving guests immediate, accurate answers.

Reservation inquiries

Answers questions about table availability, seating time windows, and party sizes. For complex requests, it creates a callback task with guest details so the hostess can respond quickly.

Menu and pricing questions

Shares menu highlights, vegetarian or allergen guidance, and pricing ranges using your TasteIQ menu data, including AI menu scans from photos.

Takeaway and delivery policies

Handles cutoff times, packaging charges, and pickup instructions. It can route callers to the right outlet or provide the correct ordering link.

Directions and parking

Offers clear directions, parking guidance, and landmark references for first-time guests. This is one of the highest volume call types for new outlets.

Special occasion notes

Captures anniversary, birthday, or seating preferences and forwards them to your team, so the experience still feels personal.

Escalation when needed

Transfers the call or triggers a WhatsApp callback when a guest asks for a manager, custom menu, or bulk booking.

Hotel & property use cases

For boutique hotels and properties: dining FAQ coverage without missing guests

Properties run multiple touchpoints: dining outlets, room service, banquet, and concierge. The AI receptionist covers the common dining questions, and routes the rest to the right team.

Dining outlet FAQs

Answers breakfast timings, menu queries, and outlet-specific schedules for coffee shops, bars, and specialty dining. Guests do not need to wait on hold to confirm simple details.

Room service routing

Guides guests on room-service availability, order timing, and minimums, then routes complex requests or delivery concerns to the right extension.

Banquet and group dining

Captures event details and gathers initial requirements before handing off to the banquet or sales team. This reduces missed leads after office hours.

Multi-outlet consistency

Ensures the spa cafe, lobby lounge, and pool bar all share accurate hours and menus. Updates in TasteIQ instantly refresh the AI receptionist responses.

Hybrid hospitality

When to keep humans in the loop

TasteIQ AI Receptionist is a hybrid model. It keeps the guest experience human by escalating when nuance, empathy, or negotiation matters. Your team stays responsible for decisions, the AI handles the routine load.

Best fit: after-hours coverage, peak call spikes, and consistent handling of repeat questions.

Human-led moments: high-value VIP guests, special event negotiations, complaint resolution, and custom menu requests.

Humans should handle

  • Negotiating group or banquet packages
  • Handling service recovery and sensitive complaints
  • Confirming special dietary or event requirements
  • VIP guest relations and personalization

AI receptionist can handle

  • Operating hours and location directions
  • Menu questions and dietary clarifications
  • Simple reservation and availability questions
  • Routing to the right outlet or department
How it works

Built on top of TasteIQ operations

The AI receptionist does not live in isolation. It uses the same menus, hours, and outlet details you already manage in TasteIQ, so updates are immediate and staff does not have to maintain two systems.

Step 1

Connect your call flow

Forward a primary number or after-hours line to the AI receptionist. You choose the hours it is active and the escalation rules.

Step 2

Feed it your menu and policies

TasteIQ imports menus from photos and updates them instantly. Operating hours, holiday changes, and dining policies stay synced.

Step 3

Define escalation contacts

Set up who receives calls for VIP requests, banquet leads, or urgent issues. Escalations can be call transfers or WhatsApp tasks.

Step 4

Track missed-call intent

Every conversation is summarized so the team knows exactly what the guest needed and can respond faster.

Integration with TasteIQ POS and hotel F&B: because the AI receptionist reads the same outlet configuration, your front desk and restaurant teams see consistent information across billing, menus, and guest communication.
