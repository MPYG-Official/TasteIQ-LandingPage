Refund & Cancellation

How cancellations and refunds work for TasteIQ plans

Cancellations

You may request cancellation of a paid plan by emailing founders@tasteiq.in or via WhatsApp support. Access continues through the paid period already invoiced unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Refunds

  • Demo and consultation requests are free and not billed unless a paid plan is confirmed.
  • Refund eligibility for subscriptions depends on the commercial agreement, plan term, and whether service delivery has begun.
  • Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method where possible, typically within 7–14 business days after approval.

How to request

Email founders@tasteiq.in with your business name, plan details, and reason. Or message us on WhatsApp.

Disputes

We aim to resolve billing concerns fairly and quickly. Contact support first so we can review invoices and usage before any chargeback.
