Definition

What is restaurant POS software?

Restaurant POS software records orders, applies taxes, processes payments, routes kitchen tickets (KOT), tracks inventory, and reports sales in real time. Unlike retail POS, it is built for dine-in, takeaway, delivery, and multi-outlet workflows.

Modern cloud platforms add branded ordering and central dashboards. International operators also need multi-currency billing and configurable tax rules (VAT, GST, sales tax, service charges) without custom development.
